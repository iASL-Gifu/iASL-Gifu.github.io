## Introduction

Hi! I am a PhD student in Computer Engineering at the University of Santiago de Compostela. I am originally from Galicia, in north-western Spain. My background combines computer engineering, video-game technology and LiDAR data processing.

## Hometown

I was born in Redondela but I studied and work in Santiago de Compostela, a historic city in Galicia best known as the final destination of the Camino de Santiago. Galicia is a green and rainy region with a strong cultural identity, its own language and excellent food.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on high-performance computing and efficient processing of LiDAR point clouds. I am particularly interested sensor calibration.

## Hobbies & Skills

Outside research, I enjoy travelling, dancing, working on personal programming projects.
